## 1. Goal and explicit non-goals

Goal: rebuild Fast English from zero as a mobile-first Persian-RTL web product (Laravel 13 + Blade + Livewire 4/Alpine + Tailwind 4.1+ + Flux Free where needed + Filament 5 staff panel + PostgreSQL + Vite + Pest/Pint + Playwright) where learners pick a short English article at a CEFR level, read it, and hear the matching audio; with manual bank-transfer receipts, subscriptions, optional placement, PWA + online TWA, and staged slices S0→S9 (scope §1–3, §21).

Non-goals for the rebuild (scope §4): bank gateway, auto/OCR receipt, SMS OTP, native iOS, store release, paid offline download, push, AI tutor/speaking/runtime AI-TTS, flashcards/SRS, full translation, word highlight, public import pipeline, chart dashboard, gamification, recommender, coupons/affiliates, multi-teacher, multi-locale UI, dark mode; plus no implementation, installs, migrations, SMTP setup, credentials, production writes, deploys, merges, or PRs in this planning turn.
